How the ranking works

₹ per $ is the effective rate: rupees received ÷ total dollars paid (including any fee, wherever the provider hides it — some deduct it from the amount you enter, others add it on top). True cost is how far that sits below the mid-market rate — the rate the banks trade at, with no margin. That gap is what the transfer really costs you, whatever the fee line says.

Where the numbers come from

official API — the provider's own public quote API. provider website — read from the provider's live calculator; standard (non-promotional) rates. indicative — third-party comparison data; treat as approximate until we quote the provider directly.

Why promos are excluded

Most apps advertise a boosted exchange rate that applies only to a first transfer, capped and one per customer. If you send money regularly, that rate is not your rate. We always quote what an ordinary repeat customer gets. Signup bonuses are real one-time savings — use them once, then come back here for the honest ranking.
