Why compare before every transfer?

The gap between the best and worst option is rarely on the fee line — it hides in the exchange rate. On a typical $1,000 transfer the spread between providers is thousands of rupees, and the ranking changes as rates move.

How the ranking works

One honest number

₹ per $ is rupees received ÷ total dollars paid — including the fee, wherever the provider hides it. Some deduct it from the amount you enter, others add it on top; we normalise both.

Benchmarked to mid-market

True cost is how far your effective rate sits below the mid-market rate — the rate banks trade at with no margin. That gap is what the transfer really costs, whatever the fee line says.

No promo tricks

Boosted first-transfer rates are excluded by design. If you send money regularly, that rate is not your rate. Every quote here is what an ordinary repeat customer gets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the mid-market exchange rate?
The rate banks and currency markets trade at, with no margin added for either side. No consumer service gives you exactly this rate — the gap between it and what you receive is the transfer's true cost, and it is the benchmark every quote here is measured against.
Why do “zero-fee” transfers still cost money?
Providers that charge no visible fee recover their margin through a worse exchange rate. A $0-fee transfer at a rate 3% below mid-market costs more than an $8-fee transfer at a near-perfect rate. That is why we rank by rupees actually received per dollar paid, never by the fee line.
Do these rankings include new-customer promotional rates?
No — deliberately. Most apps boost the exchange rate for a first transfer only, capped and one per customer. If you send money regularly, that rate is not your rate. Every quote here is the standard rate an ordinary repeat customer gets.
Can I send to an NRE account?
Not with every provider. The NRE column marks services confirmed to deposit into NRE (Non-Resident External) accounts with a ✓; a ✗ means they cannot, and ? means we have not verified it yet.
How fresh is this data?
Quotes are collected automatically every hour from provider APIs and live calculators, and the page shows the collection time under the table. Rates move constantly, so always confirm the final quote with the provider before sending.
